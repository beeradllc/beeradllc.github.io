What Services Does BeeRad Provide?

Equipment Diagnostics

Comprehensive diagnostics to identify equipment faults, warning lights, active codes, performance problems and derates.

Electrical Systems & Wiring

Electrical troubleshooting and repair for wiring, connectors, circuits, sensors and control systems.

Hydraulic Systems

Hydraulic troubleshooting, repair, maintenance and component replacement for equipment hydraulic systems.

Heating & Air Conditioning

Diagnosis and repair of equipment heating, ventilation and air-conditioning systems.

General Heavy Equipment Repair

Mobile repair and maintenance support for farm, construction and off-road equipment.

Preventive Maintenance

Maintenance support designed to help reduce downtime and keep equipment operating reliably.

Equipment We Service

BeeRad focuses on farm, construction and off-road equipment. We provide mobile diagnostics and repair when practical, so customers can avoid transporting equipment unnecessarily.

  • Farm and agricultural equipment
  • Construction equipment
  • Off-road equipment
  • Electrical and electronic systems
  • Hydraulic systems
  • Heating and air-conditioning systems

Equipment limitation: CAT off-road equipment is not currently included in BeeRad's service offering.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does BeeRad come to the equipment location?

Yes. BeeRad is a mobile service and comes to the equipment location when practical.

Can BeeRad diagnose an active equipment fault or derate?

Yes. Diagnostic service is intended to identify likely causes of active faults, performance problems and reported symptoms.

Does BeeRad repair electrical and hydraulic problems?

Yes. Electrical, wiring, connector, control-system and hydraulic troubleshooting and repair are among BeeRad's core services.

Where does BeeRad provide service?

BeeRad is based in Lubbock, Texas and serves customers in surrounding West Texas areas.
