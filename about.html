About BeeRad LLC

Built on family, hard work, and a commitment to honest, dependable service.

BeeRad LLC is a mobile heavy equipment repair and diagnostics company serving Lubbock, Texas and the surrounding West Texas area. We provide reliable service with a focus on honest work, clear communication and dependable results.

How BeeRad Began

BeeRad was built on family, hard work, and a vision for the future. The name reflects the personal foundation behind the business and the pride that goes into every repair, diagnostic, and customer interaction.

Our founder created BeeRad to build a legacy rooted in integrity, resilience, and quality service. Every job is approached with the mindset that customers deserve honest communication, dependable workmanship and a solution that supports the long-term health of their equipment.

Our Values

  • Honest service and clear communication
  • Skilled diagnostics before repairs
  • Respect for customer time and equipment
  • Dependable work that supports the field and the farm
