About BeeRad LLC

Built on family, hard work, and a vision for the future.

BeeRad LLC is your trusted partner for professional mobile equipment repair and diagnostics services in Lubbock, Texas. With years of experience, we provide fast, reliable, and honest service to keep your equipment running at its best.

How BeeRad Became BeeRad

BeeRad was built on family, hard work, and a vision for the future. The name "BeeRad" is a combination inspired by our children's names—a daily reminder of why we push ourselves to do better every day.

My husband started this company to create a legacy for our family and to show our children that dreams can come true through dedication, integrity, and perseverance. Every repair we make is done with that same purpose in mind: to build something meaningful, dependable, and lasting.

At BeeRad LLC, we believe in honest work, skilled craftsmanship, and treating every customer like family. We take pride in the trust our clients place in us and work hard to deliver the kind of service that earns that trust every single day.
