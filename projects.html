About BeeRad LLC

Built on family, hard work, and a vision for the future.

BeeRad LLC is your trusted partner for professional mobile equipment repair and diagnostics services in Lubbock, Texas. With years of experience, we provide fast, reliable, and honest solutions for farm, construction, and off-road equipment.

How BeeRad Became BeeRad

BeeRad was built on family, hard work, and a vision for the future. The name "BeeRad" is a combination inspired by our children’s names—a daily reminder of why we push ourselves and serve our customers with integrity.

My husband started this company to create a legacy for our family and to show our children that dreams can come true through dedication, integrity, and perseverance. Every repair we complete reflects that mission.

At BeeRad LLC, we believe in honest work, skilled craftsmanship, and treating every customer like family. We take pride in the trust our clients place in us and work hard to deliver dependable results.
